About Me!



Hi, I'm Prachi, an artist based in Newcastle upon Tyne, UK, with a passion for environment art, illustration, and visual storytelling.
My creative journey began through animation and digital illustration and has gradually evolved into creating immersive 3D environments and cinematic scenes. I love exploring the intersection between art and technology, transforming ideas into atmospheric worlds that invite viewers to pause, explore, and imagine.
Whether I'm sketching concepts, building environments in Unreal Engine, or experimenting with lighting and materials in Blender, I'm driven by curiosity and a desire to continually refine my craft. I enjoy learning new techniques, collaborating with others, and embracing creative challenges that push me to grow as an artist.
Thank you for taking the time to visit my website — I hope you enjoy exploring my work.
